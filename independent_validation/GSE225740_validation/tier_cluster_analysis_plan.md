# Frozen-tier and cluster assessment plan

Recorded before association/pattern calculations for this expanded analysis on 7 November 2026. The earlier 23-gene results are already known; this is an additional, explicitly scoped validation analysis, not a prospectively registered study.

Inputs: frozen `11_final_all_3845_genes.csv` and `11_final_tier1_genes.csv`; GSE225740 preprocessed 87 staged patient matrices. No reranking, rescoring, clustering, selection based on validation results or graph/manuscript changes.

1. Record availability for every one of the 3,845 genes. An unmapped or constant gene is untestable, not a zero-effect or failed-significance result. Preserve all such rows.
2. Test each measurable, nonconstant gene with two-sided Spearman against F0–F4. Primary Tier 1 family: all testable genes among the frozen 447 Tier 1 genes, BH q<0.05 and original sign concordance. Report a second BH adjustment across all testable genes among the full 3,845; use this common family for comparing tiers, to avoid unequal correction families. Prior shortlist q values across 23 remain distinct.
3. Report direction concordance, significance replication and effect summaries by frozen tier. Comparisons describe prioritisation performance; no claim of independent tier groups, confounder matching, calibrated prediction accuracy or validation of each score component. Do not add post hoc filters after viewing results.
4. For each original cluster, keep gene memberships fixed. Report available/testable gene counts, association replication under the common testing family and descriptive gene-level correlations between original five-stage profiles and validation five-stage mean log2CPM profiles. These correlations contain only five points; no gene-profile shape p values or equivalence claims.
5. Build a fixed cluster score as the equal-weight mean of the preprocessed within-cohort z scores of its available, nonconstant genes. Use patients (n=87), not genes, as units for a Spearman association of score versus stage; BH over five cluster scores. A monotonic score association does not validate a specific nonlinear cluster shape.
6. Compare original and validation cluster centroid shapes using the same available/testable genes. Original centroid is the mean frozen F0–F4 profile; validation centroid is mean fixed-cluster patient score by stage. Independently standardize each centroid over five means (ddof=0) for descriptive plotting, report Pearson shape correlation and largest absolute adjacent-stage change. No re-estimated cluster labels or ARI: independent patients, absence of re-clustering and one cohort make ARI an inappropriate primary metric here.
7. Quantify pointwise uncertainty in the unscaled validation cluster score means by 2,000 within-stage patient bootstrap samples, seed 20261109; plots/TSV disclose small F4 n=6. These intervals are not simultaneous or formal profile-equivalence tests.
8. Keep HTR7/CACNA1C low-expression caveats from prior QC. Preserve all outcomes. Cohort provenance supports separate recruitment but cannot guarantee identity-level non-overlap. Study is cross-sectional and unadjusted for unavailable demographic covariates.
