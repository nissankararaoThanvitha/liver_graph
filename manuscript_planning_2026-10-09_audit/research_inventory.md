## Preprocessing and identifier harmonisation

Parser dispatch has **three** code branches: matrix, GCT, archive; CSV versus TSV is a delimiter choice. GCT skips two headers and Description; archives derive GSM identifiers from members. Numeric columns are selected; duplicate identical raw gene rows keep the first; unparseable/missing values are dropped; zeros retained unless explicitly requested otherwise. This means “nothing silently dropped” describes mapping reports, not every preprocessing operation. Sources: pipeline/01_parse_expression.py/read_gct, read_tar, to_long, main.

HGNC approved entries provide current symbols, Entrez IDs, previous symbols and aliases. Previous/alias entries are accepted only if unambiguous and do not shadow higher-priority mappings. Ensembl version suffixes are removed; Ensembl IDs absent from HGNC pass through with blank annotation. Thus 100% Ensembl mapping is an identifier-retention result, not independent validation of every gene. `_PAR_Y` removal occurs during graph construction; different raw IDs mapping to a shared canonical gene have measurements summed. Sources: pipeline/02_map_gene_ids.py/build_lookups and resolve; pipeline/05_build_graph_all.py/main.
