The enriched snapshot was inspected on 28 October 2026. Original relationship-type counts were compared with the final combined analysis record. Progression-link endpoints and reported properties matched for all 8,044 links; the figure subgraph matched by gene identifier, annotation identifier and relationship type. These checks do not constitute independent biological validation or a complete comparison of every graph property. Dashes indicate that this table does not quantify the earlier property coverage.

\textbf{External-layer provenance.} External relationships record GtoPdb 2026.3.1; DGIdb 5, interactions downloaded in October 2026; ChEMBL REST API, October 2026; and Open Targets Platform 26.09. These identifiers are reproduced from graph properties. Source-specific retrieval, identifier matching, filtering and deduplication for the later external layer require the corresponding importer and source-file manifest to be archived with the resource. The metadata-recovery implementation distinguishes OptimusKG recovery from ChEMBL gap filling. The original reference-file manifest alone does not document the later external acquisitions.

\textbf{Coverage interpretation.} A populated approval or withdrawal flag may be true or false. A phase field does not establish current approval, availability or efficacy for liver disease. ExternalDrug is a label on a Drug subset rather than a ninth broad entity category. Missing metadata remain missing; coverage should be evaluated against the stated denominators. Drug-stage annotations and curated gene connections do not establish therapeutic efficacy.
