# Concise figure captions
Prepared from the last verified project export. Overleaf has not been edited; browser editing is unavailable. Existing labels and candidate-diagram numbering must be resolved during the planned figure/supplement revision.

## Workflow (currently Figure 1)
**Study workflow.** Processing and harmonisation of eight liver transcriptomic datasets, knowledge-graph integration, cross-study association analysis, stage-profile clustering, functional enrichment and candidate-gene prioritisation.

## Association overlap and direction (currently Figure 2)
**Fibrosis and disease-group associations.** (a) Overlap of selected gene sets. (b) Median study correlations for the 1,822 shared genes. The displayed Spearman statistic compares the two correlation vectors across genes. Selection required BH \(q<0.05\), consistent direction in all testable studies and at least three studies. Disease-group ordering denotes control/NAFL/NASH classifications, not a direct inflammation measurement.

## Clusters and stability (currently Figure 3)
**Fibrosis-stage expression patterns and stability.** (a) Individual gene profiles (grey) and cluster centroids (red), standardised across F0--F4. (b) Heatmap of the same profiles. (c) Silhouette and stability diagnostics; error bars show SD across 50 refits. Patterns describe cross-sectional stage differences, not longitudinal trajectories. Grey lines represent genes, not confidence intervals.

## Stage contrasts and enrichment (currently Figure 4)
**Adjacent-stage contrasts and functional enrichment.** (a) Substantial-gene counts, median counts from 50 matched refits and median absolute study-adjusted coefficients. Substantial differences require BH \(q<0.05\) and \(|\beta|\geq0.2\) within-study SD units. Matched refits use 30 patients per stage with fixed study composition; bars show medians without error bars. (b) Hallmark enrichment across direction groups and clusters against 14,794 genes. Colour denotes \(-\log_{10}(q)\), capped at 10; lightest cells indicate no significant enrichment.

## KG subgraph (currently Figure 5; planned supplement)
**Prioritised gene--annotation network.** Curated connections link 42 Tier 1 genes to twelve annotations through 154 relationships. Displayed genes reach at least three selected annotations, or are shortlisted genes reaching at least one. Connections represent annotation membership, not demonstrated physical or causal interactions.

## Candidate set diagram (planned main Figure 5; not yet inserted)
**Cluster membership and stage contrasts of shortlisted genes.** Five disjoint groups show the 23 candidates and their cluster-specific Tier 1 counts. Gene colours identify the adjacent-stage contrast with the largest absolute study-adjusted coefficient. The summary reports significance and effect-magnitude retention across all five leave-one-study-out refits. These are cross-sectional associations and internal robustness assessments.
